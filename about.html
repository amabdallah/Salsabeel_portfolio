About me

From the ICU bedside to the classroom

Arabic is my native language, English is fluent and Turkish is proficient. Across three countries I have worked as a bedside nurse, a clinical instructor, a lecturer and a researcher.

Academic profile

I am a nurse educator and clinical researcher with experience in critical care nursing, undergraduate nursing education, student research mentorship and clinical research. I use quantitative and qualitative methods, clinical instruction, simulation-based learning and community health outreach.

My research interests include maternal-child health, patient safety, family-centered care, culturally responsive health promotion and health equity.
