Teaching philosophy

Foster critical thinking

Students should ask why before they act. I connect pathophysiology to the patient in front of them and use questioning and case discussion to build sound judgment.

Develop clinical competence safely

Skills labs and high-fidelity simulation let students rehearse, make mistakes and improve before real patients are involved. Clinical debriefings turn each shift into learning.

Prepare compassionate nurses

Quality care is culturally responsive and family-centered. I teach students to see the person behind the diagnosis and to advocate for patient safety.
